你手上有两个工具：一个查关税配额（某国某类钢材免税额度还剩多少、用完要不要交 50% 关税），一个算碳成本（进口这批钢材的 CBAM 碳关税多少）。配额数据每天自动更新；碳成本用欧委会官方季度证书价（保持最新），你只管用。 You have two tools: one for the tariff quota (how much duty-free room is left for an origin×product, and the 50% duty once it's gone), one for the carbon cost (the CBAM charge on a steel import). Quota data updates automatically every day; the carbon cost uses the Commission's official quarterly certificate price, kept current — you just use it.

1先花两分钟搞懂两个词Two words to understand first

TRQ 配额（Tariff-Rate Quota）quota
欧盟给每个出口国、每类钢材设了一个免关税进口额度，按季度开放、先到先得。额度用完之后，再进口就要交 50% 的高关税。所以客户很关心"我的配额还剩多少、快不快用完"。 The EU gives each exporting country and steel category a duty-free import allowance, opened quarterly on a first-come, first-served basis. Once it's used up, further imports pay a 50% out-of-quota duty. So clients care a lot about "how much of my quota is left, and how fast is it going".
CBAM 碳边境关税carbon border levy
欧盟的碳关税：进口钢材要按它的碳排放买"CBAM 证书"。碳排越高、成本越高。 The EU's carbon tariff: steel imports must buy "CBAM certificates" based on embedded emissions. Higher emissions = higher cost.

两者叠加：配额内进口也要缴 CBAM，配额外还要再加 50% 关税。一句话记住——TRQ 工具看关税额度，CBAM 工具看碳成本。 They stack: in-quota imports still pay CBAM; out-of-quota adds the 50% duty on top. In one line — the TRQ tool is for the duty allowance, the CBAM tool is for the carbon cost.

3客户问我…我该怎么查"A client asks me…" — where to look

Q"土耳其热卷的配额还剩多少？用了多少了？""How much Türkiye HRC quota is left? How much is used?"
开配额追踪器 → ② 追踪表 → 顶部搜索框输 土耳其 或类别 1A 或订单号。那一行就有配额量、剩余余额、已用量、占用率。想核对官方数字，点那行红色的订单号直接跳到欧盟官方页面。 Open the tracker → ② Tracker table → type Türkiye / category 1A / the order number in the search box. That row shows quota, balance, used, % used. To verify officially, click the red order number to jump to the EU official page.
Q"这批 10 万吨超出配额了，要多交多少关税？""This 100,000 t exceeds the quota — how much extra duty?"
用③ 配额外成本测算：选订单号、输出货量和货值（欧元/吨），它自动按当前余额拆出配额内/外，配额外部分按 50% 算出额外关税和"每吨加价"。或选"自定义拆分"直接填配额内/外各多少吨。 Use ③ Out-of-quota cost estimator: pick the order, enter volume and value (EUR/t); it splits in/out of quota by the current balance and computes the 50% extra duty and per-tonne uplift. Or "Custom split" to enter in/out tonnages directly.
Q"哪些配额快用完了 / 已经用光了？""Which quotas are nearly / already gone?"
看① 配额看板：KPI 一行直接给"临界数""已用尽数"。或在追踪表点状态筛选块 临界Critical / 已用尽Exhausted 只看这些。热力图上红色格子就是紧张的。 See ① Dashboard: the KPI row shows "critical" and "exhausted" counts. Or in the table click the 临界Critical / 已用尽Exhausted status chips. Red cells on the heatmap are the tight ones.
Q"这个类别土耳其没配额了，换别的国家还有空间吗？""Türkiye is out for this category — any room from another origin?"
看热力图（看板里）：同一行（同一类别）横着看各国的颜色，绿色的就是还有余量的来源——这是它最有用的地方，一眼看出"换谁能走配额内"。 Use the heatmap (on the dashboard): read across one row (one category) — green cells are origins with room. This is its best use: instantly see which alternative origin can still clear in-quota.
Q"客户要 CBAM 碳成本报价 / 这批货碳关税多少？""A client wants a CBAM cost / how much carbon duty on this?"
开CBAM 计算器：选年份季度 → 原产地 → HS 编码/生产路线，右边立刻给出每吨 CBAM 成本和拆解。把它加到配额外关税上，就是这批货的完整额外成本。 Open the CBAM calculator: pick year/quarter → origin → HS code/route; the right panel gives the CBAM cost per tonne and breakdown. Add it to the out-of-quota duty for the full extra cost.
Q"配额什么时候重新开放？""When do quotas re-open?"
看追踪器⑤ 时间线：有下一次季度开闸的倒计时（配额每季度 1 号重开，先到先得，开闸首日是抢配额窗口）。 See ⑤ Timeline in the tracker: a countdown to the next quarterly opening (quotas reopen on the 1st each quarter, first-come-first-served; day one is the land-grab window).

5记住这三点就够了Just remember these three

  1. 数据是自动更新的——侧栏显示的日期就是最新数据日，你不用做任何维护。（官方周末不更新，周六日和周五一样是正常的。） Data updates automatically — the sidebar date is the latest data day; you maintain nothing. (Official data isn't updated on weekends, so Sat/Sun matching Friday is normal.)
  2. 这是估算，供商务参考——正式清关与申报以欧盟海关系统和当时法规为准。给客户报价时说明这一点。余额是全欧盟共享、先到先得，清关那一刻可能已更低。 These are estimates for commercial reference — actual clearance follows EU customs and the regulations in force. Say so when quoting. Balances are EU-wide, first-come-first-served, and may be lower by clearance time.
  3. 拿不准就问——遇到工具没覆盖的问题（碳钢管件、非不锈钢品类、异常数字），别猜，问 Thomas 或钢铁团队。 When unsure, ask — for anything the tools don't cover (carbon-steel tubes, non-stainless categories, odd numbers), don't guess; ask Thomas or the steel team.
