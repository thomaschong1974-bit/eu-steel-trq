③ 配额外成本测算③ Out-of-quota cost estimator

两种测算方式：按余额自动拆分——选订单号、输入计划出货量，按当前 TARIC 余额自动拆出配额内/外；自定义拆分——直接指定配额内、配额外各多少吨。超出部分按 50% 配额外关税估算额外成本。注意：余额是全 EU 共享、先到先得，清关时点的实际余额可能低于快照。 Two modes: Auto (by balance) — pick an order number and planned volume, the split follows the current TARIC balance; Custom split — set in-quota and out-of-quota tonnages directly. The excess is costed at the 50% out-of-quota duty. Note: balances are EU-wide, first-come, first-served — the balance at clearance may be lower than this snapshot.

提示：配额内进口仍需缴 CBAM（TRQ 与 CBAM 并行叠加）——CBAM 证书成本请用主计算器《钢铁 CBAM 成本计算器》测算后相加。 Note: in-quota imports still pay CBAM (TRQs stack with CBAM) — estimate certificate costs in the companion Iron & Steel CBAM Calculator and add them on top.

⑦ 方法论⑦ Methodology

已用量 = 期间配额量 − TARIC 剩余余额 (Balance)Used = period quota − TARIC balance
余额取自官方 TARIC 配额查询（DDS），单位由 kg 换算为吨；每周一、三、五同步官方快照（季度开闸期加密为每日）。Balance from the official TARIC quota consultation (DDS), converted kg → tonnes; synced Mon/Wed/Fri (daily around quarter openings).
占用率 = 已用量 ÷ 期间配额量 × 100%% used = used ÷ period quota × 100%
状态阈值：<70% 充裕 · 70–90% 偏紧 · 90–100% 临界 · ≥100% 已用尽（与 DDS "Exhausted" 状态一致）。Thresholds: <70% safe · 70–90% warning · 90–100% critical · ≥100% exhausted (matches DDS "Exhausted").
Δ 日均 = (T₀ 余额 − T₁ 余额) ÷ 间隔天数；预计用尽 = 余额 ÷ Δ 日均Δ/day = (balance T₀ − balance T₁) ÷ days; est. runout = balance ÷ Δ/day
基于快照历史（最多 14 天窗口）线性外推，仅供节奏参考；抢闸期（开闸首日）消耗高度非线性。预计用尽晚于季末则显示"可撑至季末"。Linear extrapolation over the snapshot history (≤14-day window), indicative only; draw-down is highly non-linear on opening days. If runout falls beyond quarter-end, "lasts past quarter" is shown.
配额外成本 = 超出量 × 货值 (EUR/吨) × 50%Out-of-quota cost = excess volume × value (EUR/tonne) × 50%
50% 为 Reg 2026/1384 Annex I 规定的从价配额外关税；不含 CBAM、反倾销/反补贴等其他措施。50% is the ad valorem out-of-quota duty per Reg 2026/1384 Annex I; excludes CBAM, AD/CVD and other measures.
季度结转（Art 3(3)）：季末未用余额 → 转入下一季度Quarterly rollover (Art 3(3)): unused balance → next quarter
配额用尽或停止提取后，退回数量只用于调整余额、不重新开放提取；已结转的余额对应的退回量不再二次结转。Once a quota is exhausted or drawing stopped, returned quantities only adjust the balance and do not reopen it; quantities returned after a transfer are not carried forward again.

⑧ 使用说明⑧ User guide

怎么用这个追踪器How to use this tracker

上方选择期间（配额按季度开放：Q3 = 7–9 月为当前活跃季）。表中"配额量"是该期间为每个订单号分配的免税量。 Pick a period at top (quotas open quarterly; Q3 = Jul–Sep is the current live quarter). The "Quota" column is that period's duty-free volume per order number.

"剩余余额"列为固定数据，来自每周一、三、五自动同步的官方 TARIC 余额快照（侧栏显示快照日期），不可在表内直接修改；已用量与占用率由此自动算出。点每行订单号可直达官方 TARIC 查询页核对（订单号与原产地已带好）。 The Balance column is fixed data from the Mon/Wed/Fri-synced official TARIC snapshot (snapshot date in the sidebar) and cannot be edited in the table; used amount & % used are derived from it. Click a row's order number to verify on the official TARIC consultation page (order & origin pre-filled).

提示：本工具每周一、三、五随 SMM 官方快照更新（季度开闸期加密为每日）；如需更多钢铁数据服务，请联系 SMM 钢铁团队。 Note: data refreshes Mon/Wed/Fri from SMM's official snapshot (daily around quarter openings); contact the SMM Steel Team for further steel data services.

状态判定Status thresholds

充裕 <70%Safe <70% 偏紧 70–90%Warning 70–90% 临界 90–100%Critical 90–100% 已用尽 ≥100%Exhausted ≥100% 未跟踪Not tracked

配额类型：CSQ = 国别专属配额（订单号 09.98/09.99，MFN+FTA 合并为单一订单号）；FTA–CSQ（09.95）= 国别配额用尽后 FTA 伙伴的追加竞争配额；其他国家（09.96）= 剩余共用池；FTA–其他国家（09.97）；英国→北爱尔兰（09.94）。 Quota types: CSQ = country-specific (orders 09.98/09.99, MFN+FTA merged into one order number); FTA–CSQ (09.95) = additional competitive quota for FTA partners after their CSQ is exhausted; Other countries (09.96) = shared residual pool; FTA–Other (09.97); UK→Northern Ireland (09.94).

季度滚动：某季度未用完的余额结转下一季度（Art 3(3)）；配额一旦用尽或停止提取，之后退回的数量不再重新开放。TRQ 与 CBAM 并行叠加：配额内也要缴 CBAM，配额外再加 50% 关税。 Quarterly rollover: an unused quarterly balance carries to the next quarter (Art 3(3)); once a quota is exhausted or drawing is stopped, quantities returned afterwards do not reopen it. TRQs stack with CBAM: in-quota volumes still pay CBAM; out-of-quota adds the 50% duty.
